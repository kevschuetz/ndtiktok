# Feature Research

**Domain:** Faceless, research-grounded neurodivergence (ADHD/ADHS, autism) short-form video account. TikTok first, cross-posted to YouTube Shorts and Instagram Reels, in German (DACH) or English.
**Researched:** 2026-10-24
**Confidence:** MEDIUM. The studies on content quality and engagement are peer-reviewed (HIGH). Platform-mechanics claims come from training data plus marketing blogs (MEDIUM/LOW, flagged inline). Follower counts for named accounts are point-in-time snapshots and must be re-read during the competitor scan.

"Features" here means the attributes of the content and the account that the audience and the platform reward or punish, plus the light M1 tooling that produces them.

---

## The one finding that shapes everything else

The niche has a **documented quality–engagement inversion**, and it applies in both languages:

- **English TikTok (Yeung et al., 2022, top 100 #ADHD videos):** 52% misleading, 27% personal experience, 21% useful. Only 11/100 were made by healthcare providers. Misinformation ran at 3% in professional videos and 55% in non-professional ones. The average video had about 2.8M views and 31k shares. 71% of the misleading videos presented transdiagnostic symptoms (anxiety, mood swings, relationship conflict) as ADHD-specific.
- **Karasavva et al., PLoS ONE 2025 (100 most-viewed #ADHD videos, ~500M views):** 51.3% of the symptoms described were non-specific, and only 1.4% of posts mentioned differential diagnoses. Heavier consumption correlated with overestimating ADHD prevalence.
- **German-language TikTok:** a 177-video German analysis found 68.5% of the "ADHS symptoms" shown were normal human experiences. #ADHS had about 440M views in Jan 2024.
- **Philipsen (Bonn, DGPPN 2024):** only 8% of the most-viewed ADHD self-diagnosis videos were useful, and those got **4% of the likes and 1% of the comments**.

**Implication:** sourcing on its own does not win distribution. Evidence-based content loses when it reads like a lecture. The winning feature set is **relatable, lived-experience-style hooks** (the format that dominates the feed) **plus visible sourcing and hedging** (the thing almost nobody does). Either half alone is already crowded or already losing. The validation experiment should test how much hook energy the sourcing can carry.

There is also an **underserved slot**: almost all of these studies flag misattributed transdiagnostic symptoms. A "this is human, not specifically ADHD, but here is what research says about why it can be stronger in ADHD" framing is honest, rare and on-brand. It is a candidate differentiator angle, and it maps cleanly onto the operator's hedged tone.

## Feature Landscape

### Table Stakes (Users Expect These)

Missing any of these makes the account feel amateur or inaccessible, or causes the algorithm to drop it in the first 3 seconds.

| Feature | Why Expected | Complexity | Notes |
|---------|--------------|------------|-------|
| **Hook in the first 1–2 seconds, spoken AND as on-screen text** | 40–70% of viewers leave in the first 3s (MEDIUM, industry data). TikTok shows each video to a small test cohort first, and a plateau "in the low hundreds" usually means a weak hook. | LOW | Dominant hook shapes in the niche: "POV / Some people with ADHD…", "Things nobody tells you about…", "If your child does X, it might not be defiance", "The reason you can't start tasks isn't laziness". Keep the hedge ("some people") inside the hook, not after it. |
| **Burned-in, accurate, styled captions** | ND audiences, especially ADHD viewers, use redundant visual text to support auditory processing. Neurodiverse users campaigned for captions until TikTok shipped auto-captions in April 2021, and auto-captions are judged inaccurate (CHI 2024, CSCW 2023). Many viewers also watch muted. | LOW (M1: CapCut/Captions app) → MEDIUM (M2: Whisper word timestamps) | Required, not optional. Use high contrast, 1–3 words per chunk, no flashing or strobing, stable placement inside safe zones (clear of the UI on all 3 platforms). Austrian German proper nouns and technical terms (Exekutivfunktionen, AWMF) need manual correction. |
| **Readable, calm visual layer for a faceless format** | Faceless means the visual layer carries retention. ND viewers penalize sensory overload. | MEDIUM | Typical faceless options: stock b-roll, simple illustration/comic panels, kinetic text, screen-recorded "paper highlight" shots. Avoid rapid cuts and loud SFX; this audience reads them as hostile. |
| **Clear single topic per video, 60–75s (or under 60s)** | 30–60s is the dense middle of short-form, with retention benchmarks around 40% at 30–60s and about 30% past 60s (LOW/MEDIUM, vendor benchmarks). | LOW | The brief's 60–75s targets TikTok's >1 min monetization threshold (MEDIUM: Creator Rewards needs videos over 1 min). See the monetization note: **Austria is not eligible for Creator Rewards** (LOW-MEDIUM, third-party lists dated 2026; verify). Treat length as an experiment variable only after pillar winners are known. |
| **Consistent visual identity / series branding** | Recognizable thumbnails and opening frames turn one-off viewers into followers, and series titles ("Part 3") drive profile visits. | LOW | One template: color, font, a recurring "📚 Quelle / Source" end card. |
| **Hedged, non-diagnostic language + "not a therapist" framing** | Platform policy risk (medical misinformation), legal risk (AT/DE health claims) and audience trust. Studies show self-diagnosis anxiety is the main criticism of ND TikTok, so the audience and journalists are primed to call it out. | LOW | Put a one-line disclaimer in the bio and occasionally on-screen, never as a long preamble that kills retention. |
| **Bio stating who you are, what you post and why to trust it** | Profile visit → follow conversion. Anonymous health accounts get asked "who are you?" in the comments. | LOW | e.g. "Ich lese ADHS/Autismus-Forschung und übersetze sie. Kein Therapeut. Quellen in jedem Video." An Impressum link is required in AT/DE (MedienG §25 / ECG §5). Faceless ≠ anonymous. |
| **Hashtags/keywords that match search** | TikTok increasingly behaves as a search engine. German users search "ADHS", English users "ADHD"; #ADHS, #ADHD, #neurodivergent, #autismus, #actuallyautistic. | LOW | Put the spoken keyword in the first sentence and the on-screen text (indexed). |
| **Comment moderation (manual) + crisis resource line** | Mental-health comment sections attract disclosures. Never auto-reply (project decision). | LOW | Pinned/bio crisis resources: AT Telefonseelsorge 142, DE 0800 111 0 111, plus the English equivalent. Use keyword filters to hide abuse. Manual replies only. |
| **Cross-posting to Shorts + Reels with platform-native tweaks** | Cheap extra reach. Each platform distributes independently. | LOW | Strip TikTok watermarks (Reels deprioritizes watermarked reposts — MEDIUM, widely reported). Shorts: clickable external links are not supported in Shorts (MEDIUM), so sources go in the description or pinned comment as text. |

### Differentiators (Competitive Advantage)

These line up with the Core Value: credibility through visible sourcing.

| Feature | Value Proposition | Complexity | Notes |
|---------|-------------------|------------|-------|
| **Visible sources on every video: on-screen citation + full list in pinned comment/caption** | Less than a quarter of top ADHD videos come from anyone with a credentialed or evidence basis, and source-citing is rare enough that the studies don't even report it as a category. Debunking/explainer research finds credibility comes from being a "non-typical influencer" who shows evidence (ACM CHI work on expert debunking). | MEDIUM (needs the script helper + fact-check) | Format: a 1–2s on-screen card ("Studie: Faraone et al. 2021, Neurosci Biobehav Rev") at the moment of the claim, plus the pinned comment with author/year/journal/DOI or the AWMF register number. Name guidelines plainly in German ("laut S3-Leitlinie ADHS"). **Pinned comment over caption:** captions truncate and their length competes with hashtags. |
| **"Paper-on-screen" b-roll** (highlighted abstract or guideline sentence, scrolled or zoomed) | Turns the moat into a visual. It's a faceless-friendly format that literally shows the receipt. Very few ND accounts do it. | LOW–MEDIUM | Respect copyright: show a short highlighted excerpt, not full pages. Open-access papers and AWMF guidelines (public) are safest. |
| **Male, Austrian/standard-German or male English voice** | ND TikTok/Instagram is dominated by women (ADHD-in-women, AFAB autism, "ADHS-Mama" accounts like @hey_mamaleben, @verena_eisbrenner, guardianofmind, kirmesimkopf). Men/dads/partners are thin, and clinician dad voices (ADHD Dude, Ryan Wexelblatt, LCSW) are English-only and clinician-led. | LOW (it's the operator) | This makes Pillar 4 (men/dads/partners) a structural gap rather than just an "early bet". Measure it as its own arm. |
| **Strengths framing grounded in actual evidence** | Strengths content is popular but usually unsourced ("ADHD superpowers"). The evidence for ADHD/autism strengths is mixed and narrower (e.g. some findings on creativity/divergent thinking in ADHD, attention to detail/pattern detection in autism, often with small samples). Saying exactly that ("what research does and doesn't show") is distinctive. | MEDIUM (harder grounding; the fact-check must catch overclaiming) | Highest overclaim risk of the 4 pillars. Make the fact-check stricter here. |
| **"Human, not ADHD-specific — but here's the research nuance" angle** | Directly counters the most-documented misinformation pattern (transdiagnostic symptoms presented as ADHD-specific). Media and clinicians in DACH are actively criticizing this (ZDF/funk psychologeek, Stuttgarter Zeitung, RiffReporter). | LOW–MEDIUM | Risk: it can read as gatekeeping to the community. Keep it affirming: "this is real for you; it's also common; research suggests ADHD can amplify it because…". Test as a hook variant. |
| **Parent pillar: situational "if X happens, try Y" with a source** | Parents are the highest-value audience (they buy guides). Parent content in DACH is mostly lifestyle/diary (ADHS-Mama accounts) and in English mostly clinician-led (ADHD Dude, Good Inside-style). Sourced, non-clinician, practical parent scripts are a gap. | MEDIUM | Use AWMF S3 ADHS parent-training content and Barkley-style parent-training evidence. Stay off the blacklist (no "is my child autistic?", no ABA, no medication). |
| **Named, recurring series** (e.g. "Was die Forschung sagt #12", "Dad-Edition", "Mythos oder Studie?") | Series drive profile visits, binge-watching and follows, and suit an ND audience that values predictability. | LOW | "Myth or study?" is a strong faceless format: the claim on screen, a pause, then the verdict with the source. |
| **Lived-experience vignettes (anonymized, consented) as the hook, research as the body** | Lived experience is the format the algorithm rewards (27% of the top 100 videos were personal experience; Karasavva: the majority is subjective reports). Pairing it with sourcing combines the two halves of the inversion. | LOW–MEDIUM | Consent log + anonymization checklist. Read in first person by the operator only with clear framing ("Ein Freund erzählte mir…"). |
| **Accessibility beyond captions** (no strobe, slower pace option, on-screen summary card at the end, alt text on carousels) | Signals "made by people who get it". ND commenters notice and share. | LOW | End card: a 3-bullet takeaway (helps viewers with working-memory limits and improves saves). |
| **Save-worthy payoff** (checklist, script for a conversation, "3 things to try") | Saves and shares are the go/no-go metrics, and practical, reusable content earns saves. | LOW | Each script ends on a concrete, sourced action, never a diagnosis. |

### Anti-Features (Commonly Requested, Often Problematic)

| Feature | Why Requested | Why Problematic | Alternative |
|---------|---------------|-----------------|-------------|
| **"Signs you have ADHD/autism" / symptom checklists / self-tests** | The highest-viewing format in the niche. | It is exactly what the studies identify as misleading, it implies diagnosis, it's blacklisted by the project, and it's the main thing DACH media and psychiatrists criticize. One viral hit-piece would destroy the credibility moat. | "Why X feels harder for some ADHD brains (and what research suggests helps)". Experience-led, not diagnostic. |
| **"ADHD superpower" hype** | Feels affirming and gets shares. | Overclaims beyond the evidence, and parts of the community see it as dismissive. | Evidence-calibrated strengths: "what's actually been found". |
| **AI avatar / AI female voice / AI "doctor"** | Scales production. | Project decision against it. AI-generated "doctors" giving health advice are being exposed (Science Feedback). TikTok requires AI labeling. It kills the authenticity differentiator. | Operator voice. TTS is a fallback only, and labeled. |
| **Auto-reply / DM bots / comment-to-get-link automations** | Common growth hack ("comment GUIDE and I'll DM you"). | Mental-health crisis risk (project rule). Disclosures in DMs require human handling. | Link in bio; manual replies only. |
| **Trend audio / meme-only content with no sourced claim** | Easy reach. | Dilutes positioning, and trending sounds over voiceover hurt comprehension (captions help but don't fix it). Trains the algorithm on the wrong audience. | At most a low-volume background bed under the voice. Occasional trend formats are fine *only* if they carry a sourced point. |
| **Medication / diagnosis / ABA / "is my child autistic?" topics** | High search demand. | Legal (AMG, health claims), clinical and community-conflict risk. Blacklisted in M1. | Revisit only with clinician partnership (never in M1). |
| **Long on-screen disclaimers or a lecture-style opening** | Feels responsible. | Kills the first 3 seconds, and evidence-based content already underperforms because of this style. | One hedged word in the hook + the bio disclaimer + sources in the pinned comment. |
| **Stitching/dueting misinformation to "debunk" it** | The debunk format is proven, and stitches borrow reach. | Amplifies the original. Faceless debunkers look like attackers. Creator conflict in a small DACH niche. Risky before you have an audience. | "Myth or study?" with a paraphrased claim, no stitch. Consider stitching *good* creators later (agreement + added source). |
| **Email capture / lead magnet / products in M1** | Monetization is the end goal. | Out of scope until traction + the legal checklist. Austria is not in Creator Rewards (verify). The funnel needs an Impressum and GDPR double opt-in (DSGVO). | Plan the funnel in M2. In M1, reserve the link-in-bio for the sources hub + Impressum. |
| **Posting 3×/day to "feed the algorithm"** | Common creator advice. | Breaks the 30–60 min/day budget and the fact-check discipline, and adds confounds to the experiment. | ~1/day, a fixed slot, a consistent template. |

```
Competitor scan ──decides──> Language ──decides──> Source corpus (S3 German vs English reviews), hashtags, crisis lines

Metrics log (tagged pillar/angle/hook/length) ──requires──> Consistent template (else confounded)
                                              └──feeds──> Go/no-go readout ──feeds──> M2 automation scope

Lived-experience vignettes ──requires──> Consent + anonymization log

Link-in-bio sources hub ──requires──> Impressum (AT legal) ; TikTok clickable bio link may require ≥1,000 followers (MEDIUM — verify)

Email capture / products ──requires──> Legal/tax checklist + traction (M2+)

Stitch-debunk ──conflicts──> credibility-first positioning (early) 
Symptom-checklist hooks ──conflicts──> Core Value / blacklist
Varying hook AND pillar in the same comparison ──conflicts──> a readable experiment
```

### Dependency Notes

- **The language decision blocks the source corpus and hashtags.** Run the competitor scan in week 1 before batch-scripting.
- **Fact-check output feeds two consumers:** the operator review (claim ↔ sentence) and the pinned comment (formatted citations). Design one output, two renderings.
- **The metrics log depends on template consistency.** If visuals, length and posting time all vary at once, the pillar comparison is unreadable.
- **Link in bio:** on TikTok, the clickable website link has historically required ≥1,000 followers on personal accounts, while business accounts get it earlier (MEDIUM, training data — verify). Instagram allows it immediately. Shorts don't allow clickable links, so the pinned comment carries plain-text citations. The plain-text pinned comment is the platform-agnostic source channel.

## Competitor Scan — what to measure

Goal: a data-backed **language decision** plus a **gap map**. Spend about 2–3 hours, by hand, logged in a spreadsheet.

**Sampling:** for each language, search the core terms on TikTok (ADHS / ADHD, Autismus / autism, "ADHS Eltern" / "ADHD parenting", "ADHS Männer" / "ADHD men", neurodivergent) and record the first ~15–25 distinct *recurring* creators (≥10 niche posts). Add each language's hashtag view totals (#ADHS ~440M in Jan 2024; #ADHD is in the tens of billions, so re-read both live).

**Per account, record:**

| Field | Why |
|-------|-----|
| Platform(s), language, followers, total posts | Size/saturation |
| **Median views of the last 15 posts** (not the top viral one) | Real reach; outliers mislead |
| Posts/week | Cadence norm |
| Creator type: clinician / lived experience / brand-org / faceless | Positioning crowding |
| Face vs faceless; format (talking head, VO + b-roll, text-only, comic/illustration, stitch) | Format crowding |
| **Source-citing level:** 0 none · 1 vague ("studies show") · 2 named source spoken/on-screen · 3 full citation in caption/pinned/link | **The moat check.** Expect level 0–1 to dominate. |
| Hedging/diagnostic language (symptom lists? self-tests?) | Gap + risk norm |
| Pillar/audience coverage: adults-women / adults-men / parents / partners / strengths / autism | Gap map |
| Voice gender | Male-voice gap |
| Hook style of top 3 recent videos | Hook library seed |
| Saves + shares per 1k views where visible (shares are often public; saves are visible on TikTok) | Engagement norm |
| Monetization: link-in-bio target, products, newsletter, sponsors, affiliate | Monetization map |

**Decision rule (pre-register it):** choose German if (a) fewer than ~5 DACH accounts score source-citing ≥2 *and* (b) the median-views-of-recent-posts of mid-size (10k–100k) DACH accounts is not dramatically lower than English (e.g. within ~5–10×, since the DACH market is roughly a tenth of the size). Choose English if DACH accounts at that tier show a hard reach ceiling, or if the English sourced-faceless slot is equally empty. Record the reason either way.

**Verified examples (follower counts are snapshots — re-read):**

- **DE/DACH:** kirmesimkopf / "Kirmes im Kopf" (Angelina Boerger; lived experience, book with KiWi, YouTube "Kirmes im Gehirn"; covered by ZDF funk psychologeek). nessadhs (named as a large DACH ADHS creator in German press). @hey_mamaleben (Anna Isernhinke, ADHS family, ~26k IG). @verena_eisbrenner (ADHS adult). guardianofmind (Kathi, autism in women, German). @ultrafocused "Finally Awake" (ADHS/autism explainers). **Pattern:** lived experience, female, unsourced or weakly sourced. **No sourced, male, faceless account found** (LOW confidence — not exhaustive).
- **EN:** How to ADHD (Jessica McCabe; face-led, research-informed, YouTube-first). Dani Donovan (ADHD comics/infographics, ~600k TikTok; illustration format — the faceless precedent). ADHD Alien (Pina Varnel, German-born comic artist, ~138k IG — proves faceless illustration works in this niche). ADHD Dude (Ryan Wexelblatt, LCSW; clinician dad, boys/parents — the closest analog to Pillar 3+4, but clinician and face-led).

## Validation Experiment Design (20–30 videos)

New accounts have very noisy distribution: early videos are tested on tiny cohorts, reach drifts upward as the account warms, and a single viral outlier dominates averages. The design has to survive that.

1. **One primary variable: pillar (4 arms).** Hold constant: template, voice, caption style, length band (60–75s), posting slot (same local time daily), hashtag scheme. Do **not** vary hook style independently in the same window; tag the hook type, but treat it as secondary/exploratory.
2. **Balanced rotation, not blocks.** Post in rotating order (e.g. a Latin-square across days: P1-P2-P3-P4, P2-P3-P4-P1, …) so every pillar appears every 4 days. This spreads the account-warm-up trend and day-of-week effects evenly across arms. 24 videos = 6 per pillar; 28 = 7.
3. **Ratio metrics, not raw views.** Raw views mostly measure distribution luck. Per video:
   - **Average watch time % / completion rate** (retention quality),
   - **Saves per 1k views**, **shares per 1k views**, **follows per 1k views** (value),
   - views (context only).
   Snapshot every video at a **fixed horizon** (e.g. 72h and 7d) so older videos aren't advantaged.
4. **Minimum-exposure filter.** Exclude a video's ratios from the comparison if it got fewer than ~200–300 views. Ratios on 80 views are noise. Count how many were excluded per arm (a pillar that keeps stalling below the threshold is itself a signal: weak hooks).
5. **Relative-to-own-median scoring.** Compute the account median for each ratio. For each pillar, report (a) the median of its videos' ratios and (b) the count of its videos above the account median (e.g. "5 of 6 above median on saves/1k"). Use medians over means to neutralize one viral outlier. Optionally use a bootstrap or simple sign test, but be honest: with 6–7 per arm only **large** differences (≈2× on a ratio, or ≥5/6 above median) are trustworthy. Report everything else as "inconclusive" rather than a winner.
6. **Use cross-posts as replication.** The same video on Reels and Shorts gets independent distribution. If a pillar wins on TikTok *and* on at least one other platform, confidence rises a lot at zero extra production cost.
7. **Hook testing as a second, smaller phase.** After the pillar readout, re-cut 2–4 winning-pillar scripts with 2 hook variants each (same body, different first 3s) posted a few days apart. Compare 3-second retention and average watch time.
8. **Pre-register the go/no-go rule** in the metrics log before video 1 (e.g. "winner = top pillar on the composite of saves/1k + shares/1k with ≥4/6 above median; tie-break by completion %"). This prevents post-hoc storytelling.
9. **Log qualitative signals as well:** comment themes (questions = topic backlog), "source?" requests, and pushback/criticism. In this niche a single "this is misinformation" comment thread matters more than a ratio.

## Platform / monetization notes that affect features

- **TikTok Creator Rewards:** third-party 2026 lists exclude **Austria** (eligible: US, UK, DE, FR, JP, KR, BR, MX) (LOW-MEDIUM; verify on TikTok Creator Academy). Eligibility follows the creator's account region, so platform payouts from TikTok may be zero for an AT-based creator. This makes the 60–75s ">1 min" design choice less important for payouts, and products/sponsors/YouTube more important. Feed this into the legal/tax checklist item.
- **YouTube Shorts:** low RPM, but a funnel to long-form. Shorts have no clickable external links.
- **Instagram Reels:** link in bio available from day one. Carousels (sourced infographic slides) are a cheap cross-format repurpose that ND audiences save heavily (Dani Donovan/ADHD Alien precedent).
